# Roadmap

## P0 — Business review of the first trial

Available in v0.2: explicit intake/unknowns, focused tasks, minimum data requests, dependency checks, separate task/effect acceptance and evidence-bound feedback. These mechanisms are implemented; actual business confirmation and operating results below remain pending.

Next: business owner confirms goals, reconciles the original platform exports, selects the highest-value action and returns execution records. Dependency: authorized private source data. Acceptance: source conflicts explained, action has owner and pre-agreed observation window, costs and stop conditions; outcomes are separated from hypotheses.

## P1 — Native export adapters

Next: collect authorized anonymized LuoPan/QianChuan export samples and field definitions for the actual product version. Add explicit mappings rather than fuzzy column guesses. Acceptance: account, session ID, timezone, time range, attribution and null semantics survive mapping; canonical-to-source lineage is independently checked.

## P1 — Contribution and cohort analysis

Next: add costs, fee definitions, payment cohorts and repeated refund snapshots. Dependency: original exports and financial agreement on contribution. Acceptance: same-age refunds compared, refund accrual shown, no claim of profit without complete costs.

## P2 — Content and advertising deep dive

Next: add video/creative IDs, approved media/transcripts and live time segments. Acceptance: traceable creative-to-session linkage, paid/natural scope distinguished, no invented algorithm causes, controlled test results logged.

## P2 — Broader validation

Next: obtain business-reviewed anonymized examples and test with new store schemas and missing modules. Acceptance: improve substantive action quality under review; regression coverage for new adapters; no claim that unit tests establish operating returns.
